# Acme Corporate Travel & Business Expense Policy

## Scope and Principles
This policy applies to all business travel, entertainment, and operational expenditures incurred by Acme Global employees. All expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and incurred for legitimate business purposes.

## Travel Bookings

### Air Travel
1. **Domestic Flights:** Employees must book the lowest standard economy class fare. Non-stop flights may be selected if the fare difference is under $100 compared to connecting flights.
2. **International Flights:** Flights exceeding **7 hours of continuous flight duration** are eligible for Premium Economy. Flights exceeding **10 hours of continuous flight duration** are eligible for Business Class with prior VP approval.
3. **Booking Lead Time:** Flights must be booked at least **14 calendar days in advance** through our corporate travel portal (Navan).

### Hotel Accommodations
- Standard room reservations should not exceed the nightly city allowance:
  - Tier 1 Cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): Up to **$320 per night** excluding taxes.
  - Tier 2 Cities (Austin, Chicago, Berlin, Toronto): Up to **$220 per night** excluding taxes.
  - Other Locations: Up to **$175 per night** excluding taxes.

### Ground Transportation
- Employees are encouraged to use public transit or approved ride-sharing services (Uber, Lyft, taxis).
- Rental cars are permitted only when public transit or ride-sharing is cost-ineffective or unavailable. Compact or mid-size vehicles must be selected.
- Personal vehicle mileage for business travel is reimbursed at the standard IRS federal mileage rate (**$0.67 per mile** in 2026).

## Meals and Per Diem Allowances
- **Daily Meal Allowance:** Employees traveling on business receive a per diem meal cap of **$85 per day** ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $45 dinner).
- **Client Meals:** Client entertainment dinners must have a pre-approved business purpose and must not exceed **$125 per attendee** including gratuity.
- **Alcohol:** Moderate alcohol consumption during business meals is reimbursable, capped at two drinks per attendee.

## Expense Submission & Reimbursement
1. **Receipt Requirement:** Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual expense item exceeding **$25.00 USD**. Credit card payment slips lacking itemization are insufficient.
2. **Submission Deadline:** All expense reports must be submitted via the expense portal within **30 days** of the transaction or travel return date. Expenses submitted past 60 days will not be reimbursed.
3. **Approval Hierarchy:**
   - Expenses under $1,000 require Direct Manager approval.
   - Expenses between $1,000 and $5,000 require Department Director approval.
   - Expenses above $5,000 require VP or CFO approval.
