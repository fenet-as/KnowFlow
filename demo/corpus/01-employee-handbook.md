# Acme Global - Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## Welcome to Acme Global
Welcome to Acme Global! This handbook provides a summary of company policies, employee benefits, and workplace expectations. All full-time and part-time employees are expected to familiarize themselves with these guidelines.

## Working Hours and Remote Work Policy
Acme Global supports a flexible hybrid workplace model.

### Core Hours
Our core collaboration hours across all offices are **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time**. Team members should be accessible via Slack and email during these hours for meetings and synchronous team discussions. Outside of core hours, employees may schedule their remaining workday as desired.

### Remote Work Eligibility
1. **Hybrid Schedule:** Full-time employees in good standing may work remotely up to **three days per week**, with two designated anchor days spent in their local office.
2. **Full Remote:** Fully remote status requires vice-presidential approval and an approved home office workspace ergonomics check.
3. **Workspace Stipend:** Every newly onboarded employee receives a one-time **$500 home workspace setup stipend** to purchase ergonomic equipment (chair, desk, monitor, or peripherals). Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of starting.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves

### Annual PTO Accrual
Full-time employees accrue **20 days of paid vacation** per calendar year, accruing at a rate of 1.67 days per full calendar month of service.
- **Carryover Limit:** Up to **5 unused vacation days** may be rolled over into the following calendar year. Any additional unused days beyond 5 are forfeited on March 31 unless prohibited by local statutory law.
- **Notice Period:** For vacation requests longer than three consecutive business days, employees must submit requests via the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

### Sick and Wellness Leave
Employees receive **10 paid sick and wellness days** per calendar year, awarded on January 1. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member. A doctor's note is required only for absences extending beyond four consecutive business days.

### Parental Leave
Acme Global provides **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** to all eligible parents (birth, adoption, or foster placement) after six months of continuous service. Parental leave may be taken continuously or in two equal blocks within the child's first 12 months.

### Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to **5 consecutive paid business days** for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to **3 days** for extended family.

## Health and Wellness Benefits
- **Medical, Vision, and Dental:** Acme Global pays 90% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums for comprehensive health coverage.
- **Wellness Allowance:** Employees receive a monthly wellness reimbursement of **$75** eligible for gym memberships, fitness trackers, wellness apps, or physical sports leagues.
- **Mental Health:** Employees and dependents have access to six free counseling sessions per calendar year through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

## Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment
Acme Global maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding harassment, discrimination, or retaliation based on race, gender, religion, national origin, sexual orientation, disability, or age. Any concerns may be reported confidentially to People Operations or via our anonymous compliance hotline.
